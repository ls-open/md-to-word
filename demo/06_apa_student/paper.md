%% 副标题 The Effect of Spacing on Reading Speed

# Introduction

Reading speed depends on typography; this reports a small spacing experiment.

## Background

Double spacing slows reading but improves proofreading accuracy.

### A note on measurement

Reading time was measured in seconds per page.

#### Equipment

A stopwatch and printed sheets were used.

##### Calibration

The stopwatch was checked against a reference clock before each session.

# Method

## Participants

Twenty students took part.

## Materials

Each participant read two passages set in twelve-point Times New Roman.

%% 表格题注 **Table 1** Mean Reading Time by Condition
| Condition | Mean time (s) | SD |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Single spaced | 92.4 | 8.1 |
| Double spaced | 108.7 | 9.6 |

%% 表注 *Note.* Times include page turns.

# Results

Double spacing was associated with a longer mean reading time.

# Discussion

The result is consistent with earlier work, though the sample is small.
